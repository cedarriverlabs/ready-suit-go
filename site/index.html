Texts to your phone

Before you book.

Short answers. The long version is on the pass you agree to at the locker.

Do I have to be there in the morning?

No. Morning is the drive to the shop. You should already be gone, or asleep.

What if something never made it into the bag?

Then it isn’t ours. Shoes by the dance floor stay shoes by the dance floor. We only take the pieces you check off in the photo.

Are you the rental shop?

No. The rental contract stays yours. Stains and tears stay yours. We move the bag from the venue to the shop named on that contract.

Where do I pay?

$50 a suit, when you book. Not at the locker, and not the next day.

Can one person book the whole party?

Yes. Add a line for each suit. Each person still confirms their own bag before it leaves with us.
